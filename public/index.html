Keenan Oliver is a designer and creative director from Lentegeur, Mitchells Plain, living and working in Cape Town.

He began drawing as a child, studied graphics at Tygerberg Art Centre and later Marketing at CPUT. His practice developed through photography, publishing, clothing, music, hospitality and cultural projects—fields that taught him to think about how an image enters the world, who it addresses and what remains after it circulates.

Oliver’s work moves between visual identity, publication, exhibition and space. It is shaped by Cape Town’s social geography, by collaboration, and by an ongoing interest in how cultural memory is made, held and shared.

He is the founder and creative director of Artefacts Office, a practice working with brands, artists and institutions.
