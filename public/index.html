Keenan
Oliver

A designer and creative director from Lentegeur, Mitchells Plain, working across visual identity, publication, exhibition and space.

His practice is shaped by collaboration, Cape Town’s social geography and an interest in how cultural memory is made, circulated and kept. He is the founder and creative director of Artefacts Office.
